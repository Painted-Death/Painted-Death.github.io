Vesper

Vesper is a personal voice assistant that one person built for his own use. It runs on his own Windows PC and is not offered to anyone else.

Among other things, Vesper can read his Google Calendar aloud, check when he is free, and add, move or delete events when he asks it to (it reads each change back and waits for a yes first). It also sets reminders as calendar events so they alert on his phone.

Read how Vesper handles Google data in the privacy policy.
