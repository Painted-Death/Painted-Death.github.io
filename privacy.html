Vesper privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Vesper is a personal voice assistant used by one person, its builder, on his own PC. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data Vesper uses

With the owner's permission, Vesper uses the Google Calendar API (the calendar.events permission) on the owner's main calendar only, to:

  • read upcoming events, so it can tell him what is on and when he is free;
  • add, change or delete events when he asks, after reading the change back and getting a yes;
  • add reminders as short calendar events with an alert.

If the owner also grants it, Vesper uses the Google Tasks API (the tasks permission) to keep his shopping and to-do lists: reading them, adding items and ticking items off when he asks.

How it is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is saved in a file on the owner's own PC. Calendar events and list items are fetched when needed and are not saved anywhere else. To answer questions about them, the relevant details are sent to the AI model that powers Vesper's replies (Anthropic's Claude API) and are not kept by Vesper afterwards.

Sharing

Vesper does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. Vesper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Vesper's access at any time from his Google Account (Security, Third-party apps and services) and by deleting the token file from his PC.
